Safety Center

🛡️

Your safety comes first

Matchify is built to help you meet people confidently. Here's how we keep the community safe — and what to do if something feels off.

Report & Block

If someone makes you uncomfortable, you can block them instantly or file a report — reports are anonymous and reviewed by our safety team.

Community Guidelines

✅
Be who you say you are — use real, recent photos
🤝
Treat every match with respect and kindness
🔞
Matchify is strictly for adults 18 and over
🚫
No harassment, hate speech, or unsolicited explicit content

Dating Safety Tips

📍
Meet in a public place for the first few dates
🗣️
Tell a friend where you're going and who you're meeting
💳
Never send money or financial info to someone you haven't met
🚗
Arrange your own transportation to and from the date

Need Help Now?

If you're in immediate danger, contact local emergency services right away.
